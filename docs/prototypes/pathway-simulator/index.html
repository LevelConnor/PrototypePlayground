Prototype

Pathway Simulator

One student, one plan. Turn a knob and the whole timeline recompiles — nothing is stored with a date on it.

What this is

A working model of the scheduling engine: a content catalog of task templates with relative or fixed timing rules, compiled against this student's facts and a calendar of real-world anchors. Click any task to mark it done.

What this isn't

Authoritative. Test dates, aid deadlines and application windows here are plausible stand-ins so the mechanics are visible. Production needs a sourced, re-verified calendar table.
