You may be eligible for more federal aid than you think! Don't miss out because of a wrong answer, a missing form, or a missed deadline. Our Level All FAFSA tool walks you through exactly what you need to do to unlock that aid.

The FAFSA opens around October 1 each year. Be ready!
Some aid is first-come, first-served, which means it may be awarded in the order applications arrive. Two students with identical finances may get different results because one filed in October and the other filed in April.

This tool takes about 3 minutes, and no data is submitted or stored. You still file the real FAFSA at studentaid.gov.

It's not just for federal aid
The FAFSA also unlocks direct aid from states, colleges, and private institutions. Even if you don't think you qualify for government aid, file it.
